Professional Services

Leveraging certified expertise to offer a comprehensive range of IT technical services for home and business users.

Network Implementation & Support

Design, configuration, and troubleshooting of Local Area Networks (LAN) and Wide Area Networks (WAN). Expertise in routing protocols (OSPF, EIGRP), switching, VLANs, and InterVLAN routing (CCNA/Net+ knowledge).

IT Hardware & Software Support

Complete system maintenance, including installing, upgrading, configuring, and troubleshooting PC hardware, operating systems (Windows), mobile devices, and peripherals (A+ expertise).

Cybersecurity & System Hardening

Implementing security controls, managing access lists, vulnerability scanning, configuring secure wireless setups, and advising on threat mitigation and device hardening (Security+ expertise).

Server Administration & Recovery

Basic server setup, storage management, security implementation, and developing disaster recovery and business continuity plans (Server+ knowledge).

Technical Consulting & Audits

Providing expert advice on technology purchasing, system optimization, network best practices, and security audits for small to medium-sized businesses and home offices (SOHO).
