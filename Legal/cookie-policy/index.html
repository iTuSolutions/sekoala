Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you browse websites. They help remember your preferences, keep your session safe, and improve site performance.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use essential cookies and lightweight analytics to:

  • Remember your preferred theme selection (Light Mode vs. Dark Mode).
  • Ensure fast loading speeds across zero-hosting serverless architecture.
  • Gather POPIA-compliant, anonymized visitor statistics to optimize system performance.

3. Managing Cookie Settings

You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your web browser's security settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact dark theme persistence and performance optimizations.
