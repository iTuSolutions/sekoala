Key Diagnostic Procedures & Root-Cause Triage

PSU & Multimeter Voltage Auditing

Tested 12V, 5V, and 3.3V power rails under load using a digital multimeter to identify voltage drops causing spontaneous reboot loops. Replaced degraded capacitors within failing ATX power supply units.

System Lockup & Memory Isolation

Isolated hard system freezes during heavy utilization through MemTest86 diagnostic passes. Identified single-bit memory address errors, reseated DIMM channels, and verified motherboard VRM thermal health.

Physical Cable Pinout & TDR Testing

Used RJ45 cable testers and Time-Domain Reflectometers (TDR) to resolve packet drop issues. Located split pairs, excessive crosstalk, and damaged Cat6 patch cables, re-crimping ends to T568B standards.

Thermal Throttling & S.M.A.R.T. Audits

Monitored S.M.A.R.T. storage telemetry to identify reallocated sector counts before drive failure. Reapplied thermal interface material and adjusted fan curves to eliminate CPU thermal throttling.
